FAC Cloud is installed on this site and its MCP server is live. An AI assistant you connect to it — Claude, ChatGPT or your own — can now read and act on your Frappe data, inside each user's existing permissions.

You manage all of it from FAC Admin: which tools an assistant may use, which plugins are active, and who has access.

Turn on FAC Chat when you're ready

FAC Chat is the chat experience for your own team, built into this site. It stays switched off until an administrator enables it in FAC Admin. Once it is on, your team can ask things like:

"Show me my top 10 customers by revenue"

"Which sales orders are still pending?"

"Summarise last month's sales for me"
